Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Short version: RoastPrep has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no third-party trackers. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Game progress stays in your browser. At most, we count anonymous totals to learn which episodes work.

Who we are

RoastPrep (roastprep.com) is a vocabulary game. You can reach us at hello@roastprep.com.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice, or location.
  • No accounts or log-ins, and no way for players to message each other.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics companies, no social-media pixels.
  • No cookies.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Which episodes you finished, stars, and words you've reviewed.
  • Settings such as Chinese hints and auto-play.
  • Pet names and pet photos you choose to add. Photos are shrunk and saved on the device only; you can remove them in the pet settings.
  • A random experiment group (0 or 1) used to test two ways of marking new words. It is not linked to any person or device ID.

To erase all of it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Anonymous counts

To improve episodes, the game may send small anonymous messages such as “episode 3 started”, “episode 3 finished”, an episode rating (1–4), which joke got a laugh, whether a question was answered right on the first try, and the experiment group (0 or 1). These messages:

  • contain no name, email, account, device ID, cookie, or other identifier;
  • are added into running totals (for example, “episode 3 finished 120 times”); individual messages are not stored;
  • are not used to build profiles, for advertising, or sold or shared.

We do not store IP addresses. Like every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under its own privacy policy.

Children

RoastPrep is made for students in grades 7–11, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game has no accounts, forms or chat, there is no way for a child to submit personal information to us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
